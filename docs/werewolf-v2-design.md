# Werewolf v2: what we are building

1. **Flow:** pick, intro, roles, night, dawn, discussion, vote, verdict, then night again; a dead hunter adds a shot before the game goes on. The rules decide every step. The host only narrates.
2. **Pick:** the TV shows the village and any phone can tap. "Let Marlowe decide" is the default and fires by itself after 15 s. A toggle restarts the 15 s.
3. **Role sets (1 wolf per about 4 players, scored with Ultimate Werewolf's balance values):** 4: wolf, seer, 2 villagers. 5: wolf, seer, 3 villagers. 6: wolf, seer, 4 villagers. 7: 2 wolves, seer, doctor, 3 villagers. 8: 2 wolves, seer, doctor, 4 villagers. 9: 2 wolves, seer, doctor, hunter, 4 villagers. 10: 3 wolves, seer, doctor, hunter, 4 villagers.
4. **Toggles, 6 or more players only:** seer, doctor, hunter. A small table moves too far on one extra role, so 4 and 5 stay preset. Six keeps one wolf because two wolves can win after a single wrong vote.
5. **Intro:** the TV and every phone show the same three rules with "Got it". It ends when everyone taps, or at 30 s. Marlowe says it in 25 words or fewer.
6. **Roles:** the card opens face up on the phone with the role and what to do. Tap hides and shows it. It ends when everyone taps "I've seen it", or at 25 s. In every later phase the card stays but starts hidden, as a small strip.
7. **Night:** one shared window of 45 s, no separate wake order, because eyes are closed and every action is a silent tap. Wolf, seer and doctor act on their phones. Once all have chosen, the night holds 6 s more, so changing your mind works and timing tells nobody much.
8. **Night rules:** wolves see each other's picks; a split is settled at random. The doctor may save themselves but not the same person two nights running. A saved victim means "everyone survived", and nobody says why.
9. **Dawn:** the TV turns over the result, and the role if reveal is on. The seer's finding goes to the seer's phone only.
10. **Discussion:** up to 3 minutes. It ends on a "Ready to vote" majority of the living, on Marlowe's ruling, or at the timer. The talk control stays free: the card is a strip and the chip keeps its corner.
11. **Vote and ties:** the live tally stays on the TV, with place-card ticks. Everyone alive votes and can change until the last vote is in. The first tie goes to one 30 s runoff between the tied players. A second tie means nobody is out. Timeouts count the votes that are in.
12. **Hunter:** on death by any cause, the hunter gets 20 s to shoot one living player. No shot if the time runs out. The win check waits until after the shot.
13. **The eliminated:** the phone says they're out and shows who is still in and every role revealed so far. They keep their own card and make one changeable ghost guess at the wolf, shown at the end. They see only public facts, so no secret reaches them.
14. **Host:** one line per phase, 25 words at most, lantern voice, numbers only from the facts it is given. It never hears a role until it's public, a night choice, or a finding. Secrets stay in rules code and player-scoped facts.
15. **TV and phone polish:** paper moon and waking eyes at night, a card turned over at dawn and at a verdict, a tally that marks the winner, roles turned over one by one at the end. The talk control becomes a small chip in the corner that opens only while recording; "Talk" is the one word on it.
